### 1.1 What Do We Call This Process?
The process executed over this intensive development cycle is formally codified as:

1. **Workflow Externalization (or Workflow-to-Artifact Materialization):**  
   The systematic translation of implicit mental workflows, operational heuristics, and ad-hoc scripting into version-controlled, reproducible, deterministic software repositories, machine-readable contracts, and epistemic evidence artifacts.

   Building the tools, governance models, and automation infrastructure that build, verify, and orchestrate other tools.

   The definitive milestone marking the transition from tool creation to tooling consumption.

## 3. The 2026-09-16 Milestone Declaration: "The Long Pause"

As of **2026-09-16**, aggressive repository proliferation, architecture churn, and hypothetical feature engineering are declared **FROZEN**.

### 3.1 Rules of the Freeze (`GOV-FREEZE-V1`)
1. **Zero Unearned Projects:** No new repositories or speculative tool branches shall be initiated without explicit empirical demand.
2. **Infrastructure Consumption First:** The current toolchain (`super-nlm`, `antigravity`, `github-pilot`, `windows-pilot`) serves as live daily driver infrastructure for academic, research, and technical workflows.
3. **Friction-Driven Evolution:** Future modifications are strictly deferred until genuine operational friction is experienced and logged during actual utilization.
4. **Legibility Preservation:** The existing 6-repo portfolio spine remains frozen as the authoritative representation of engineering capability.
