## 3. Daily Learnings & Conceptual Nodes
- **Statutory Authority Discrepancy**: The Attorney General portal's English PDF (`ag.gov.np`) was finalized in 2072-11-16 (First Amendment only), lacking the crucial 2077 Second Amendment map update in Schedule 3. Ground truth for digital products must strictly reference the Nepal Law Commission (`lawcommission.gov.np`) Bhadra 2081 edition.
- **Pedagogical Shift (Rote vs. Applied Civics)**: CDC's *सामाजिक अध्ययन तथा जीवनोपयोगी शिक्षा* curriculum does not evaluate article numbers or rote legal clauses; it tests real-world dilemma resolution, constitutional morality, and structured writing formats (*संवाद, सम्पादकीय, मन्तव्य*). Products targeting schools must focus on application rather than statutory recitation.
- **Anchor-Card Methodology**: Simplifying legal terms without retaining the original anchor word cripples students in formal examinations. The optimal model maintains the formal anchor term, delivers 5th-grade plain-language translation, and grounds it with a "Why It Matters" real-world check.
- **The Symbiotic OCR Data Flywheel**: Rather than labeling scans manually, scraped digital Acts provide authentic domain sentences. Rendering these on Colab via `trdg` with realistic government fonts (Preeti, Kantipur, Kalimati) produces 50k+ labeled image patches in minutes, enabling fast TrOCR fine-tuning to crack older scanned gazettes.
- **Compounding Code Infrastructure**: The 125-year Bikram Sambat date dataset embedded months ago into `AaradhyaDT.github.io` for hero date toggling (`BS_YEARS`) was ported directly into Python (`sim/nepali_calendar.py`), instantly solving temporal alignment across the entire statutory history of Nepal (BS 1975–2099 / AD 1918–2043) with zero external dependencies.
- **Repeal vs. Savings Invariant**: The presence of *“खारेजी र बचाउ”* (Repeal and Savings) in statutory text is a standard legislative clause where an active act supersedes older enactments; treating substring matches as repeals misclassifies active statutes as dead law. Context-aware evidence extraction is mandatory.
- **Post-Write Disk Read Verification**: Computing SHA-256 in RAM before writing avoids partial file creation, but true zero-drift persistence requires an explicit `os.fsync()` followed by re-reading the byte stream directly from disk to verify the physical block checksum before committing database ledger transactions.
- **Robots.txt Float Tolerances**: Python's standard `urllib.robotparser` strictly requires decimal integer strings (`isdecimal()`) for `crawl-delay`, silently ignoring floating-point seconds (e.g. 2.5s, 3.5s). Robust scrapers must extract float delays directly to avoid over-aggressive request rates.
- **The "Preeti in Vector PDF" Trap**: Thousands of Nepali public documents are digital PDFs formatted using legacy fonts mapped to Latin-1 ASCII. Standard text extraction dumps gibberish. Font-table inspection via PyMuPDF combined with AST transcoding recovers clean Unicode text at $>50\times$ the speed of visual OCR with near-zero error.
- **Cryptographic Trust Boundary Invariant**: Never disable TLS verification (`verify=False`) to bypass poorly maintained `.gov.np` certificates. Degrade gracefully by quarantining invalid certificates as auditable security events (`INV-SEC-TLS-001`).
- **Single-Node Execution Discipline**: Defer multi-node distributed clusters (Jetson, Mac mini, secondary PCs) until single-node workstation limits (RTX 3060) are empirically reached (`INV-FLEET-001`), eliminating distributed state overhead during exploration.
