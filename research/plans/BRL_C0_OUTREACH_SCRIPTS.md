# Brainstorm Research Laboratory — Cohort 0 Outreach & Communication Scripts

This playbook contains exact, battle-tested communication scripts for inviting, onboarding, and managing Cohort 0 candidates.

---

## 1. The 3-Minute Casual Verbal Pitch (In-Person / Campus)

*Use this when speaking casually at KEC cafeteria, makerspace, or hallway:*

> *"Hey [Name], I wanted to run something by you. For the next 8 weeks, I'm launching an experimental research fellowship track under Brainstorm Research Laboratory.*
>
> *Instead of chasing vague, unpaid generic internships where you get stuck doing random busywork, we're taking a tiny group of 3 to 5 people to build verifiable engineering artifacts—actual reproducible systems, data pipelines, and research dossiers with permanent public authorship and Git commit proofs.*
>
> *It's about 5 to 8 hours a week, fully asynchronous with KEC makerspace sprints on weekends, structured as bite-sized quests. You'll work directly with our verification tools, NotebookLM research engines, and real code.*
>
> *I'm keeping the initial circle very tight with people whose work ethic I already trust. If you're interested, let me know and I'll send over the short intake form to match your hardware and quest preferences."*

---

## 2. The 1-Paragraph DM Template (WhatsApp / Telegram)

*Use this when reaching out digitally:*

> *"Hey [Name]! Hope your semester is going well. Over the next 8 weeks, I'm running an experimental research fellowship track under Brainstorm Research Lab. Instead of hunting for vague generic internships, we're taking a closed group of 3–5 people to build verifiable engineering artifacts—reproducible systems, data pipelines, and research reports with permanent public authorship in our Git ledger.*
>
> *It's 5–8 hrs/week, fully async with KEC makerspace touchpoints, structured as progressive quests. I'm keeping the circle very tight with trusted peers. If you'd like to be part of Cohort 0, let me know and I'll send over the diagnostic intake form!"*

---

## 3. The Follow-Up (Sending the Intake Form)

*Send immediately once they express interest:*

> *"Awesome! Here's the private intake form: [INSERT_GOOGLE_FORM_LINK]*
>
> *It takes about 3 minutes. It audits your local OS/GPU setup, lets you pick your top quest preferences, and checks your exam dates so we don't schedule deliverables during your exam weeks. Once you submit, I'll provision your GitHub access and send over Quest 0!"*

---

## 4. The WhatsApp Group Pinned Message (`BRL — Cohort 0`)

*Pin this in the WhatsApp group once all 3–5 candidates are onboarded:*

```text
🚀 Welcome to Brainstorm Research Laboratory (Cohort 0)

Our focus is simple: build verifiable, reproducible engineering artifacts with permanent public attribution.

📌 Essential Hubs:
• Repository: https://github.com/Aaradhya-Dev-Tamrakar/brainstorm
• Shared Datasets (Google Drive): [INSERT_GDRIVE_LINK]
• Fellowship Calendar: [INSERT_GCAL_LINK]
• Program Charter: research/plans/PLAN-BRL-001_BRAINSTORM_RESEARCH_LAB_CHARTER.md
• Contributor Agreement: research/plans/BRL_CONTRIBUTOR_AGREEMENT.md

🎯 First Task (Quest 0):
Read research/plans/BRL_QUEST_0_DRY_RUN.md.
Clone repo, make branch c0/<username>/quest-0, add your profile in research/fellows/, run .\audit.bat, and submit your PR!

⚠️ Core Communication Rule:
Zero technical questions in private DMs! Post all errors, Git issues, and questions right here in the group or open a GitHub Issue.
If coursework or exams get overwhelming at any point, just message [PAUSE]—no guilt, no penalty. Let's build!
```

---

## 5. The Graceful Pause Response

*Send if a candidate types `[PAUSE]` due to exam or project load:*

> *"Acknowledged and paused, [Name]! Your quest is frozen cleanly. Good luck with your exams/deadlines. Whenever you're ready to spin back up, just ping me here. Your previous commits and authorship standing remain 100% intact."*
